## Brand & Style

This design system crafts an intimate, tactile workspace for personal reflection, capture, and thought organization. It bridges the quiet, distraction-free refinement of bespoke editorial stationery with the speed and kinetic directness required for rapid mobile capture. 

### Brand Personality & Emotional Tone
- **Cultivated & Serene:** The visual presence echoes fine uncoated Japanese paper, dense pigment inks, and bookbinding craftsmanship. The canvas invites deliberate thought without clinical sterility.
- **Immediate & Agile:** Thought capture occurs without cognitive friction. Transitions feel immediate, actions are close to the thumb, and spatial arrangements remain fluid.
- **Quiet Authority:** Chrome and interface noise recede entirely behind content. Subtle contrast differences, nuanced warmth, and precise proportions establish order instead of loud containment lines.

### Design Aesthetic: Tactile Editorial Warmth
The interface relies on an editorial-minimalist framework blended with tactile skeuomorphic cues:
- Cream and parchment tonal shifts establish layered depth without synthetic drop shadows.
- Micro-interactions emulate the physics of paper cards sliding, stacking, and resting on an uncluttered drafting table.
- Accents do not shout; they stain and tint like watercolor washes and archival book cloth markers.

## Colors

The palette avoids stark digital blacks and pure cool whites in favor of high-grade paper stock and rich charcoal soot inks.

### Canvas & Surfaces
- **Canvas Base (`#FBFBFA`):** The primary backdrop evoking unbleached rag paper.
- **Recessed Surface (`#F4F3EF`):** Used for search bars, folder groupings, and pinned drawers.
- **Card Elevated (`#FFFFFF`):** High-grade smooth paper sheet surface for active note tiles and editor sheets.
- **Card Hairline (`#EBE8E1`):** A soft, warm-gray boundary applied at 1px to anchor cards against the canvas without creating harsh borders.

### Ink Hierarchy
- **Deep Ink Primary (`#1C1B1A`):** Pure readability for headlines, active notes, and body prose.
- **Warm Ink Secondary (`#57534E`):** Mid-tone archival ink for metadata, timestamps, and secondary labels.
- **Faint Ink Tertiary (`#A8A29E`):** Placeholder text, divider rules, and inactive states.

### Editorial Accents & Category Tints
- **Ochre / Amber Primary (`#D97706`):** The signature editorial highlighter; used for quick-capture action triggers, active tab indicators, and search highlights.
- **Warm Chamois Secondary (`#E0A96D`):** Gentle tag chips, bookmarks, and audio transcription states.
- **Subtle Sage (`#8A9A86`):** Reading lists, mindful notes, and archive states.
- **Soft Peach (`#F4B8A5`):** High-priority alerts, voice memo highlights, and pin badges.
- **Subtle Lavender (`#B8B4D4`):** Research, quotes, and creative drafts.
- **Wash Backgrounds:** Every category accent maps to an ultra-soft 12% opacity tint for card tagging and category chip fills.

## Typography

The typography system pairs **Newsreader** (a sharp, humanist optical-size serif for editorial structure and emotional breathing room) with **Plus Jakarta Sans** (a clean, warm, highly legible geometric sans-serif tuned for mobile utility).

### Typographic Hierarchy & Roles
- **Newsreader (Headlines & Note Titles):** Used for large app titles, notebook covers, and individual note header titles. It brings literary dignity, making daily entries feel permanent and considered.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans (Body & System Chrome):** Used for note body copy, checklists, search inputs, tags, and navigation labels. Its humanistic curves avoid the clinical austerity of standard system fonts while maintaining legibility at tiny sizes.
- **Line Heights:** Deliberately airy (1.5x - 1.6x on body copy) to create clear optical scanning paths across asymmetric card streams.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy centers on tactile responsiveness and dynamic masonry cards, prioritizing one-handed thumb ergonomics.

### Mobile Grid & Reflow
- **Mobile (Phone Portrait):** Uses a dynamic 2-column staggered masonry grid or a single-column structured feed with `0.75rem` (`12px`) gutters and `1rem` (`16px`) outer screen margins.
- **Tablet / Large Foldables:** Expands to an asymmetric 3-to-4 column canvas with a fixed navigation rail, widening gutters to `1rem` (`16px`) and margins to `1.5rem` (`24px`).
- **Masonry Rhythms:** Card heights adjust strictly to content density without rigid square clamping, respecting the spontaneous length of thoughts.

### Padding and Safe Insets
- **Bottom Navigation Clearance:** Always add an additional `80px` padding buffer to scroll views to guarantee card visibility above floating quick-capture docks.
- **Thumb Reach Zone:** Frequent actions (new text note, voice memo, image attachment, checklist) reside within the lower 25% of the mobile viewport.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy abandons high-contrast synthetic drops in favor of physical paper stacking, diffuse warm shadows, and micro-borders.

### Stacking Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Base Canvas):** Background `#FBFBFA`. Completely flat.
- **Tier 1 (Resting Note Cards):** Crisp `#FFFFFF` surface enclosed by a 1px `#EBE8E1` hairline border, accompanied by a resting shadow: `0px 2px 6px -1px rgba(28, 27, 26, 0.03), 0px 1px 3px 0px rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.02)`.
- **Tier 2 (Floating Action Dock & Pinned Cards):** Lifted `3px` above canvas with `0px 8px 24px -4px rgba(28, 27, 26, 0.06), 0px 2px 6px -1px rgba(28, 27, 26, 0.04)`.
- **Tier 3 (Modals, Context Menus & Full Sheets):** Edge-to-edge floating paper sheets with deep ambient dispersal: `0px 20px 32px -8px rgba(28, 27, 26, 0.08)`.

### Tactile Feedback
When notes are pressed or reordered, elevation smoothly morphs: cards scale to `0.98` scale on touch-down, then lift to Tier 2 on long-press drag, casting a softened warm ambient shadow tinted with ochre undertones.

## Shapes

The interface embraces organic, comfortable silhouettes that mimic rounded stationery, index cards, and smooth desk tools.

### Radius Assignments
- **Note Cards:** `18px` to `20px` corner rounding for a friendly, pocketable index-card feel.
- **Floating Action Bar (FAB Dock):** Fully rounded pill geometry (`9999px` / shape level 3 hybrid) for ergonomic thumb docking.
- **Interactive Controls (Inputs, Tags, Buttons):** `12px` to `14px` border radius, keeping touch targets soft and inviting.
- **Modals & Bottom Sheets:** `24px` top-edge curvature.

## Components

### 1. Note Cards
- **Visuals:** Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) surface, 1px `#EBE8E1` border, `18px` radius, padded with `space-md` internally.
- **Content Flow:** Optional image thumbnail pinned flush to the card top with clipped top corners, followed by a Newsreader title (`headline-sm`), truncated body text (`body-sm`), and a bottom metadata row displaying time and pastel tag pills.
- **Color Washes:** Cards with category colors use a 90% opacity wash of the tint with an amplified border matching the category hue at 20% opacity.

### 2. Floating Action Dock (Quick-Capture Hub)
- **Geometry:** Horizontal floating pill bar centered `16px` above the bottom safe area.
- **Structure:** Encased in translucent warm white (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)` with `16px` backdrop-filter blur) and a 1px border (`rgba(235, 232, 225, 0.8)`).
- **Controls:** Left-to-right icon actions: Checklist, Audio, Photo, Sketch, punctuated on the right by a solid Ochre (`#D97706`) circular primary button with a pure white plus icon.

### 3. Chips & Category Filters
- **Resting:** Background `#F4F3EF`, label ink `#57534E`, zero border, `9999px` radius, padding `6px 14px`.
- **Active:** Deep ink fill `#1C1B1A` with canvas-tinted white text `#FBFBFA`, or tinted pastel fills matching specific user-curated folders.

### 4. Interactive Lists & Checkboxes
- **Checkboxes:** Unchecked state uses a subtle `1.5px` border in `#A8A29E` with an `8px` corner radius. Checked state transitions with a soft spring to `#D97706` fill containing a crisp white tick; the attached text strikes through with `#A8A29E` and drops to 70% opacity.
- **Reorder Handles:** Two subtle horizontal warm gray bars revealed on swipe or edit-mode.

### 5. Input Fields & Quick-Search Bar
- **Search Bar:** Pill-shaped `#F4F3EF` background, zero border, integrated magnifying glass in `#A8A29E`, placeholder text in `body-md` Newsreader italic style for a literary, welcoming invitation.
- **Editor Canvas:** Full bleeds without visible box containers. Note titles display directly in large Newsreader typeface over canvas, separating seamlessly into body text via natural baseline spacing.
